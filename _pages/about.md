I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the School of [Computer Science and Engineering](https://www.unsw.edu.au/engineering/our-schools/computer-science-and-engineering) at the [University of New South Wales](https://www.unsw.edu.au/), working with Professor [Yulei Sui](https://yuleisui.github.io/). My current research centers on **Responsible AI** and **agentic workflows** for building trustworthy, verifiable AI systems. I am the main designer and developer of [SVF-tools/ACT](https://github.com/SVF-tools/ACT/graphs/contributors?all=1), an end-to-end neural network verification and optimization toolkit built on the [SVF](https://github.com/SVF-tools/SVF) framework.

Previously, I earned my PhD at UNSW (2022–2025) within [CSIRO's Data61](https://www.csiro.au/en/) Responsible AI program, with my thesis [*Order-Leading Branch-and-Bound for Neural Network Verification*](http://hdl.handle.net/1959.4/106703), focused on verifying deep neural networks and improving their robustness. During my PhD I was also guided by [Dr. Dilum Bandara](https://people.csiro.au/B/D/dilum-bandara) and [Dr. Shiping Chen](https://people.csiro.au/C/S/Shiping-Chen) at Data61.

My overarching objective is to **close the gap between legacy, conventional workflows and an automated, dynamic, and trustworthy cycle**.

Beyond my formal research, I am a self-confessed enthusiast of large language models (LLMs) — I love building agentic tools and pushing LLM-driven coding and research workflows to their limits in my day-to-day work, as the token counter below attests.
